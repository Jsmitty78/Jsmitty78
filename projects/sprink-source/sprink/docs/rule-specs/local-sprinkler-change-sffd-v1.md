# Local sprinkler-change review: SFFD submittal requirements

Status: primary-source requirements mapped for the bounded local-change workflow; executable coverage is being integrated separately. These source clauses support change-scoped drawing, material, support, and calculation review. They do not define a complete permit review, certify a design, or replace the adopted NFPA 13 text and project-specific authority decisions.

## Primary sources and scope

- [San Francisco Fire Department AB 2.04, Fire Sprinkler Submittals (2025)](https://sf-fire.org/media/4169), 15-page bulletin. References below use the printed page numbers and section identifiers on the bulletin.
- [San Francisco Fire Department AB 4.15, Attachments and Anchors (2025)](https://sf-fire.org/media/3979), one page. References use its printed section identifiers.

The listed AB 2.04 sections were visually checked in the official PDF at printed pages 4–10. AB 4.15 was visually checked at printed page 1. This mapping is restricted to facts and artifacts the local-change slice can compare or request. A source requirement can require documentation without giving this evaluator enough information to approve the design.

## AB 2.04 clauses relevant to the changed scope

| Clause | Source condition | Bounded implication for this change review | Limit that remains |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| I.14(A), p.4 | A modification includes removing unused excess piping. | Preserve removed pipe as a material/change item instead of omitting it from the proposal record. | The bulletin does not define a route-generation or optimal-removal algorithm. |
| I.14(C), p.4 | Sprinkler relocation is to follow an approved plan. | Compare proposed head locations with the approved baseline and flag changed locations as requiring an appropriately revised approved plan. | A proposal cannot be treated as already approved; the evaluator does not grant approval. |
| I.14(D), p.4 | A threaded sprinkler removed from a fitting or welded is not to be reinstalled. | If the change explicitly reuses a head with either confirmed removal history, report the source conflict; do not invent a waiver. | Unconfirmed removal history is unknown. The clause does not decide all other reuse/condition questions. |
| I.14(E), p.4 | A field installation that differs from the approved plan may require recalculation, considering new piping and fittings. | Keep proposed geometry distinct from actual field-deviation facts. Confirmed deviation can trigger a recalculation review request. | “May” does not make deviation alone an automatic recalculation requirement, failure, or approval. Residual decision is manual. |
| I.15, p.4 | When sprinklers and/or piping are added to an existing system, submittal plans show associated existing configuration, including connection to the existing riser. | Require/identify existing layout and riser tie-in context only when both existing-system scope and added sprinklers/piping are confirmed. | Do not apply this addition-specific condition automatically to a pure relocation/reroute. Unknown classification remains unknown. |
| I.16, p.5 | Component submittals include manufacturers' cut sheets; specially listed equipment has manufacturer instructions and technical data, including descriptions, applications, and limitations. | Attach or request source product documents for selected pipe, fittings, sprinkler, and other changed components. Use them only for the product fields they actually establish. | A catalog sheet alone does not establish system suitability or every connector/pressure/dimension fact. |
| I.18(B), p.5 | Plans show occupancy/use classifications per NFPA 13 §4.3 for each system; examples highlight spaces that may be overlooked. | Keep affected room/use/hazard classification linked to the affected head/pipe neighborhood and expose missing classification for review. | This source section does not provide the full NFPA hazard-classification rules. Do not infer the classification from room names. |
| I.19(D), p.6 | Drawings identify fitting types/details, joints, welds, bends, and locations; shop-weld details apply when shop welding is used. | Keep each proposed fitting and connection type and its location visible; request joint/weld detail where applicable. | It is a submittal-content requirement, not a product compatibility or joint-strength oracle. |
| I.19(E)(1)–(3), p.6 | Piping plans identify pipe type and wall/schedule, pipe/support locations, nominal sizes, and cutting lengths using center-to-center dimensions. | Compare explicit material, schedule, nominal size, support locations, route centerline/cut quantities and drawing detail against the approved baseline. | “Center-to-center” is a drawing dimension convention; the bulletin does not supply fitting takeout, thread engagement, cut tolerance, or a universal cut-length formula. Unknown connection makeup stays unknown. |
| I.19(E)(4), p.7 | The piping plan is separate from the reflected ceiling plan. The RCP identifies heads and relevant ceiling/structural/duct/light features and obstruction context. | Keep pipe layout and sprinkler-only RCP as separate artifacts; associate affected heads with the ceiling features and ducts used in local review. | A clear modeled pipe route does not establish head spacing, discharge obstruction, coverage, or RCP completeness. |
| I.21(A)–(F), p.9 | Submittals show support/bracing/sleeve locations and orientation; hanger/brace details, fasteners and substrates; end-of-line restraints and flexible-coupling information as applicable. | Diff support references with affected piping and request the applicable details for changed/new supports and accessible pipe. | Presence of a support ID/detail does not prove load, spacing, capacity, structural attachment, or adequacy. NFPA 13 §18.5 criteria remain source-pending here. |
| I.21(B), p.9 | Sway-brace load calculations are referenced for new systems and modifications/TI for exposed/accessible piping in the work area, with the bulletin's existing-brace condition. | When that applicability is confirmed, request the corresponding brace-load documentation and preserve existing-brace evidence for review. | Do not implement or claim the numeric §18.5 location/spacing/capacity checks without the governing source and full context. |
| I.22(A)(1), p.9 | Hydraulic calculations are required for all new sprinkler systems, subject to the referenced applicable code for NFPA 13R/13D. | If the project is confirmed to be a new system in the supported scope, require calculation documentation. | This slice does not establish which full code pathway or calculation method applies. |
| I.22(A)(2)(a)–(c), p.10 | Existing-system modifications/TIs require hydraulic calculations when hydraulically remote area is changed/modified, hazard is changed to a more conservative class, or water-supply routing/backflow assembly is modified/replaced. | Each confirmed explicit trigger independently marks calculations required. Missing trigger facts stay unknown. | Do not substitute a length delta, fitting count, head move, or intuition for one of these named triggers. |
| I.22(A)(2)(d), p.10 | Depending on the sprinkler-system work, calculations may be required case-by-case. | Preserve a distinct human-review branch when no explicit trigger resolves the decision. | A case-by-case direction is not inferred from the source; it cannot waive an explicit trigger and does not itself prove hydraulic adequacy. |
| I.22(B), p.10 | When hydraulic calculations are required, the bulletin specifies the required professional signature/seal for the submittal. | Include the required calculation artifact/signature request in the change-scoped submittal checklist when the recalculation branch requires it. | The evaluator cannot sign, seal, or assess the calculation's technical adequacy. |
| I.22(C)–(H), p.10 | Calculation submittal includes dated flow-test data, hydraulic data plate information, a supply/demand graph, matching reference points/remote area, design data, and pressure/flow at a common point, as described by each item. | Preserve calculation document/revision and affected entity/reference-point lineage; request required supporting artifacts when recalculation is triggered. | Do not regenerate, validate, or claim hydraulic sufficiency without the governing design inputs, calculations, and complete NFPA rules. I.22(C)'s separate date rule is specified in the SF submittal packet. |
| I.22(J), p.10 | Listed examples associate fitness centers, gymnasiums, and certain electrical rooms (excluding transformer vaults) with OH1. | A confirmed space/use classification may be surfaced for human hazard review when relevant to the affected area. | The examples do not establish a universal rule for all rooms, mixed uses, stricter hazard choices, or exceptions. Do not turn “not these examples” into an automatic failure. |

## Independent outcome oracles

These cases specify source-backed expected outcomes for the named review item only. They are not an overall compliance verdict.

| Case | Confirmed facts/evidence | Expected result |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Head relocation | Proposed head location differs from the identified approved baseline; current proposal has not been approved | Mark revised approved plan as required/review-needed under I.14(C); do not mark the proposed location approved |
| Same head location | No sprinkler relocation is identified and the plan/revision scope is confirmed | I.14(C) relocation item is not triggered; this says nothing about other submittal requirements |
| Removed threaded head reuse | A selected reused head is confirmed to have been removed from a fitting or welded | Conflict/fail for I.14(D) reuse condition |
| Unknown removal history | Reuse is proposed but removal method/history is absent or unconfirmed | Unknown/manual review; do not assume the head was never removed |
| Existing system, confirmed added pipe | Existing system and addition of piping or sprinklers are both confirmed | Request associated configuration and existing-riser connection information under I.15 |
| Pure reroute | Existing system is confirmed; no sprinklers or piping are added, only relocation/reroute | I.15 addition-specific condition is not triggered; still review other applicable clauses |
| Field deviation | As-built installation differs from approved revision | Recalculation review may be required under I.14(E); manual determination, not automatic failure or automatic recalculation |
| New system | Project is independently confirmed as a new sprinkler system in this supported scope | Hydraulic calculation artifact required under I.22(A)(1), subject to the referenced applicable NFPA 13R/13D provisions |
| Explicit existing-system hydraulic trigger | For existing-system TI, confirmed remote area change, more-conservative hazard, or supply-route/backflow assembly change | Recalculation required under I.22(A)(2)(a)–(c); a “no calculation” case-by-case note cannot override it |
| No explicit trigger, decision absent | Existing-system modification scope is confirmed, none of the named I.22(A)(2)(a)–(c) triggers is established, and no human case-by-case direction is present | Unknown/manual review for I.22(A)(2)(d); do not infer not-required |
| Material takeout incomplete | Drawing supplies center-to-center length but product connection makeup/takeout is unknown | Preserve center-to-center plan quantity; physical cut length remains unknown |
| Pipe layout and RCP conflated | One drawing mixes sprinkler-only RCP and pipe routing without separate artifacts | Do not treat the combined artifact as proof of both distinct deliverables under I.19(E)(4) |
| Support moved | Pipe route/support attachment changes in exposed/accessible work area | Request applicable support/bracing submittal detail and assess whether brace-load documents are triggered; no load/capacity pass from geometry alone |
| New powder-driven attachment | New hanger or sway brace is confirmed to use a powder-driven stud | Conflict/fail for AB 4.15 I.3 |
| Existing powder-driven attachment retained | Existing stud confirmed; pipe not moved/altered and hanger holds properly | Eligible to use I.2 retention condition only; not a general attachment-adequacy pass |
| Concrete fastener qualification absent | Concrete hanger/sway brace uses an unidentified or unverified fastener | Unknown for I.4; never infer qualification |

## Input and evidence boundary

Approved baseline drawing and revision, proposed plan, proposed/as-built phase, changed head/pipe/fitting/support IDs, room-use classification, change/addition classification, any field deviation, and baseline calculation reference points must remain separate facts. Proposal facts require current revisioned drawing evidence; actual installation/deviation facts require installation/as-built evidence. A source link or manufacturer datasheet is useful only for the particular product field it states. Unreadable, stale, superseded, foreign-project, wrong-subject, or model-only assertions remain unknown.

## Explicitly unresolved

This mapping does not implement NFPA 13 spacing, coverage, obstruction, support/bracing dimensions or loads, pipe/fitting joint makeup, hydraulic computation, or the complete AB 2.04 submittal checklist. It does not determine whether a change is acceptable to SFFD. NFPA 13 full text and applicable project-specific review are needed before claiming those outcomes. Unknown engineering data must remain unknown rather than filled from a synthetic calculation fixture.
