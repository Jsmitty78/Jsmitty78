## Pair comparison boundary

The two sources support a candidate size-screening match at NPS 1. The fitting sheet identifies ASME B1.20.1 threads, but the WFS-121824 submittal does not specify an actual selected pipe-end thread form or connection. Do not borrow the separate WFS-101025 sheet's threading-suitability statement to fill that gap: it is a different product data-sheet identifier. This does **not** explicitly confirm that the selected pipe end was threaded to the matching thread form or that a particular pipe and elbow were tested/listed together. Preserve pipe connection standard, fitting connection details, and selected product identity as independent evidence. A nominal size match alone is not a verified connection.

The pipe data sheet states 500 psi for 1–4 in Schedule 40, with no service-temperature condition in that statement; the elbow's Class 150 pressure rating varies with temperature. These values can be compared only after the exact pipe product, system design pressure, and service-fluid temperature are confirmed. Because the pipe rating's temperature conditions and the exact threaded joint compatibility are not established by the sheet pair, an end-to-end pressure/connection conclusion remains unknown. The sprinkler's nominal temperature classification is a different fact and cannot be substituted for pipe fluid temperature.

The elbow table reports Size A = 1.5 in, but the pair dossier does not use this number as a fitting takeout. Neither sheet establishes the actual installation's pipe-end datum, threaded engagement, field-cut allowance, fitting depth, sealant layer, or fit-up tolerance. The installation page provides a hand-tight/wrench-turn instruction, not an axial thread-makeup distance. Center-to-center plan dimensions can be reported independently; pipe cut length stays `unknown` until the connection-specific datum and makeup are confirmed from an applicable source or measured plan input.

The Wheatland sheet's UL/FM statement applies to its described pipe product line and sizes. The Anvil sheet directs readers elsewhere for listing/approval details; this pair alone does not establish the elbow's UL/FM listing, local acceptance, SFFD acceptance, or system-level suitability. Do not propagate the pipe's listing claim to the elbow or joint.

## Scope for implementation

The first local-change implementation may expose these values for side-by-side candidate comparison and flag missing inputs. It may compare exact identity, NPS, material, source-stated thread standard, and pressure only when a separately evidenced value exists on both sides and applicable service conditions are known. Do not combine the different WFS-101025 and WFS-121824 sheets as though they were one product revision. No generic listing inference, thread compatibility inference, cut takeout, hydraulic loss, or installation tolerance is authorized by this dossier. New product selections require current manufacturer documents tied to their actual revision/document IDs.
