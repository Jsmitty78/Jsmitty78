# DEMO-M-4 Modifications to an installed CPVC system

## DEMO-M-4.1 Cutting into an existing line
Drain the system before cutting. Cut the pipe square with a tool made for CPVC, and remove burrs before joining.

Do not pressurize a modified joint until the cement has cured for the time given in the cure schedule for the pipe size and temperature.

## DEMO-M-4.2 Heat sources
Keep CPVC pipe at least 150 mm from hot-air supply outlets and other heat-producing surfaces.

<!-- page: 40 -->
## DEMO-M-4.3 Supporting a relocated section
Support a relocated CPVC section so that no joint carries the weight of the pipe while the cement cures. Add a support within 300 mm of each new fitting.
