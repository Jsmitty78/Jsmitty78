# DEMO-SF Local amendments to the demonstration standard

## DEMO-SF-9.1.1 Hangers near added fittings
DEMO-9.1.1 is amended to read: A hanger shall be installed within 300 mm of each fitting added to a relocated branch line.

## DEMO-SF-8.2.4 Notice to the fire department
A route change to a branch line in an occupied building shall be reported to the fire department plan reviewer before the pipe is modified.
