# DEMO-FP-07 Field route changes

## DEMO-FP-07.1 Before changing a route
1. Stop work on the affected branch line.
2. Photograph the obstruction and the existing pipe.
3. Measure the obstruction and the space around it, and write the measurements on the drawing mark-up.
4. Call the foreman before cutting any pipe.

## DEMO-FP-07.2 Who may approve
The foreman may approve a reroute of 1 m or less without a request for information. Longer reroutes go to the project engineer.
