# DEMO-3 Definitions

## DEMO-3.1 Branch line.
The pipes that supply sprinklers, either directly or through sprigs, drops or arm-overs.

## DEMO-3.2 Obstruction.
A duct, beam, light fixture, piece of equipment or other building element that is in the path of a pipe run or of a sprinkler's discharge.

## DEMO-3.3 Route change.
A relocation of an installed or approved pipe run that changes its centerline, its fittings or its support points.

# DEMO-8 Installation around obstructions

## DEMO-8.2 Rerouting a branch line around an obstruction
DEMO-8.2.1 Where an obstruction prevents a branch line from being installed as shown on the approved drawings, the route change shall be shown on a revised drawing before the pipe is modified.

DEMO-8.2.2 The rerouted pipe shall keep the clearance to the obstruction given in Table DEMO-8.2.

DEMO-8.2.3 Hangers for the rerouted pipe shall be installed in accordance with DEMO-9.1.

Exception No. 1: Where the obstruction is 600 mm wide or less, measured along the original pipe centerline, the route change shall be permitted to be recorded on the as-built drawing instead of a revised drawing, provided no more than four fittings are added.

<!-- page: 42 -->
## Table DEMO-8.2 Minimum clearance between rerouted pipe and an obstruction
| Pipe nominal size | Minimum clearance |
|---|---|
| NPS 1 to NPS 2 | 100 mm |
| Larger than NPS 2 | 150 mm |

Note 1: Clearance is measured from the outside surface of the pipe to the nearest face of the obstruction.

# DEMO-9 Hangers and supports

## DEMO-9.1 Hangers for relocated pipe
DEMO-9.1.1 A hanger shall be installed within 600 mm of each fitting added to a relocated branch line.

DEMO-9.1.2 The distance between hangers on relocated pipe shall not exceed the values in Table DEMO-9.1.

## Table DEMO-9.1 Maximum distance between hangers on relocated pipe
| Pipe material | NPS 1 |
|---|---|
| CPVC | 1.8 m |
| Steel | 3.6 m |

# DEMO-10 Testing

## DEMO-10.4 Testing after a modification
Where a pipe run is modified, the modified portion shall be hydrostatically tested at not less than 1[?]0 psi for 2 hours before it is returned to service.

# DEMO-11 Records

## DEMO-11.9 Records of route changes
A record of every route change shall be kept with the system documents and shall identify the revised drawing or as-built drawing that shows it.
