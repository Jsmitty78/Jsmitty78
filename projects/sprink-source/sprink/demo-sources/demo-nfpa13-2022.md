# DEMO-3 Definitions

## DEMO-3.1 Branch line.
The pipes that supply sprinklers, either directly or through sprigs, drops or arm-overs.

## DEMO-3.2 Obstruction.
A duct, beam, light fixture, piece of equipment or other building element that is in the path of a pipe run or of a sprinkler's discharge.

# DEMO-8 Installation around obstructions

## DEMO-8.2 Rerouting a branch line around an obstruction
DEMO-8.2.1 Where an obstruction prevents a branch line from being installed as shown on the approved drawings, the route change shall be shown on a revised drawing before the pipe is modified.

DEMO-8.2.2 The rerouted pipe shall keep the clearance to the obstruction given in Table DEMO-8.2.

Exception No. 1: Where the obstruction is 300 mm wide or less, measured along the original pipe centerline, the route change shall be permitted to be recorded on the as-built drawing instead of a revised drawing.

<!-- page: 39 -->
## Table DEMO-8.2 Minimum clearance between rerouted pipe and an obstruction
| Pipe nominal size | Minimum clearance |
|---|---|
| NPS 1 to NPS 2 | 75 mm |
| Larger than NPS 2 | 100 mm |

# DEMO-9 Hangers and supports

## DEMO-9.1 Hangers for relocated pipe
DEMO-9.1.1 A hanger shall be installed within 900 mm of each fitting added to a relocated branch line.
